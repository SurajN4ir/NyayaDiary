Tenant Eviction Rights

My landlord gave me a 2-day verbal notice to leave the apartment in Bangalore. What are my rights?

account_balance

Summary

A 2-day verbal notice is generally invalid under the Karnataka Rent Control Act, 2001. A landlord must provide written notice, and the duration depends on the lease agreement (typically 1 month). You cannot be forcibly evicted without a court order.

gavel Karnataka Rent Control Act, 2001 description Transfer of Property Act, 1882

Immediate Steps

  1. Do not leave immediately. A verbal notice holds no legal weight.
  2. Demand written notice. Request that the landlord communicate the eviction notice in writing, specifying the reason.
  3. Review your lease. Check the termination clause in your rental agreement for the required notice period.
  4. File a police complaint. If the landlord attempts physical force, locks you out, or cuts off essential utilities (water/electricity), go to the nearest police station immediately.
